     - A toaster's speed comes from the same `r`: 0–24 gives 1, 25–49 gives 2, 50–74 gives 3.

     - Otherwise it moves 1 px further left if it is above the obstacle, or 1 px less far left if not.
     - If the nudged position still collides, the flyer stays at its base-step position.
